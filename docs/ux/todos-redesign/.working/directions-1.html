Todos redesign: 3 directions

Your 4 pain points are solved in all three: (1) every card collapses and expands, (2) a toolbar with Expand all / Collapse all, (3) progress % and count are big, (4) sub-items use a round check. Directions differ in personality and density. Pick one; board and calendar below follow the pick.

Checkbox vs radio: a radio means "choose one of several". Sub-items are independent, so they are not radios. I use the round check (like Things and Apple Reminders): same visual family as the parent's round tick, but smaller and filled green when done.

A · Soft cards

Airy cards. Big bold % with a thick bar under the title. Sub-items listed with round checks. Best for scanning a few rich items.
